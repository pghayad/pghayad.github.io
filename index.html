About Me

I was raised in the Philadelphia area, went to DC for college, picked up Computer Science after college, worked as a software engineer in industry for a few years, and now, I've been teaching and mentoring students in CS and Software Engineering for the past 5+ years. It is my ultimate and true passion in life to help people reach their goals and potentials in these fields.

My Teaching Philosophy

I believe in my students so much. I believe that they can achieve way more than they think, and I so enjoy the challenge of getting them to subscribe to such a mindset. Asking them questions and allowing for back-and-forth exchanges about various technical concepts creates free-flowing sessions that combine strict coding with a flexible infusion of creativity.
