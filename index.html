About Me

My Blog

Hello, my name is Paul. Over the past 5 years, I have dedicated myself to the instruction of Computer Science and Software Engineering at the highest-level. I take pride in the experiences that my students share with me, how happy they are to be introduced the magic of coding!

Some of the courses I have taught include: Web Application Development (graduate-level), Design and Analysis of Algorithms, Computer Architecture, Introduction to Java, as well as several introductory courses in HTML, JavaScript, React, Node, Python, Java, C++, C, and the like.

Although my strengths mostly lie in front-end development, I possess the skills to work on full-stack web application projects. As you peruse my portfolio, I just want to thank you for visiting. Please feel free to get in touch via any of the options listed at the bottom of this page. Happy Coding!
